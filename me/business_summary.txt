Furn Teta – Lebanese Bakery & Catering

Furn Teta ("Grandma's Oven") is a family-run Lebanese bakery in Hamra, Beirut,
founded in 2021 by siblings Rami and Lina Haddad. Our mission is to bring
authentic, wood-fired Lebanese baking to everyday life, made fresh every
morning using our grandmother's recipes and locally sourced ingredients.

What we offer:
- Fresh bakery: manouche (za'atar $2, cheese $3, mixed $3.50, kishk $3),
  lahm b'ajeen ($3), fatayer (spinach or cheese, $1 each), ka'ak ($1.50).
- Catering: breakfast platters for offices, events, and university clubs.
  Small platter (10 people) $45, Large platter (25 people) $100.
  Catering orders need at least 48 hours' notice.
- Weekend baking workshops: learn to make manouche from scratch,
  $25 per person, Saturdays 10am-12pm, max 8 people.

Hours: Monday-Saturday 6:30am-2:00pm. Closed Sundays.
Location: Bliss Street, Hamra, near AUB Main Gate.
Delivery: within Hamra and Ras Beirut only, via phone or WhatsApp.

Team:
- Rami Haddad (Co-founder, Head Baker): 15 years of experience in
  traditional wood-fired baking.
- Lina Haddad (Co-founder, Operations & Catering): handles all catering
  orders, events, and partnerships.

What makes us different: everything is baked in a real wood-fired saj
and oven, dough is made fresh daily (never frozen), and za'atar is sourced
directly from family farms in the South.
